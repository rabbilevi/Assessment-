Stage 2: Sexual Desire & Attraction

A follow-up self-reflection assessment designed to help distinguish a longstanding asexual-spectrum pattern from acquired low desire or primarily responsive/contextual desire.

How to answer: Rate each statement from 0–4: 0 = never / not at all, 1 = rarely, 2 = sometimes, 3 = often, 4 = very strongly / consistently. Answer according to your actual history, not what you think you should feel.
Important: This is not a validated diagnostic instrument and cannot establish an orientation or medical diagnosis. On submission, responses and the calculated summary are emailed to the form owner through Google Apps Script.
